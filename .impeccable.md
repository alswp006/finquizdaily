# .impeccable.md

## Design Context

### Users
20-30s Korean professionals and fintech enthusiasts in the Toss ecosystem who want to build financial literacy without heavy textbook learning. They're motivated by daily habits, peer comparison, and feeling rewarded for consistent engagement—solving 3 questions takes ~2 minutes and fits naturally into their morning routine.

### Brand Personality
**Approachable, Rewarding, Clear.** Tone is encouraging and conversational—celebrate wins (streaks, badges), normalize financial learning as fun micro-habits rather than daunting study, and always explain *why* a financial concept matters. Match Toss's trusted, modern voice.

### Aesthetic Direction
TDS (Toss Design System) 준수. @toss/tds-mobile 컴포넌트 전용. 커스텀 미적 방향 없음.

### UX Principles
- **Habit loop in <2 minutes**: Frictionless quiz flow—minimal navigation, card-based layout, instant feedback on answers.
- **Transparent progress tracking**: Streak counter, badge unlocks, and weekly leaderboard rank visible at all times so users see impact of daily visits.
- **Reward clarity**: Ad-gated deeper content (5 bonus questions + full explanations) is explicitly labeled and worth the 30-second watch—no surprise paywalls.
- **Peer motivation**: One-tap share card (score + rank) drives return visits through social proof and friendly competition.
